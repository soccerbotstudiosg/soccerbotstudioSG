## Two-tab master booking check

Use two tabs in the same browser session. Choose eligible future times and an approved payment fixture. Record the original times, amount, invoice and deadlines for comparison. Keep the first tab open throughout.

1. In A, submit a guest checkout with an address known to reach checkout and receive a definitive email rejection. Press browser Back immediately after submission. Check for one [email rejection notice](../booking-flows.md#rejected-contact-email). Correct and validate the email, submit again, then press browser Back immediately.
2. On Contact details, edit the name and press the site’s Back. On Continue to pay, note the original checkout. Use browser Back to reach Calendar, select another slot and trainer, then choose Continue payment. Verify the original booking, amount and deadline. Where authorized, compare the original provider record for contact and slot changes.
3. Check [Leave this booking?](../booking-flows.md#leaving-a-booking) and choose Stay. Open HitPay and return before the deadline. Let the **first checkout expire on the studio’s Continue to pay page**. After verified unpaid release, expect Calendar with one expiry notice. Check that the expired slot is absent immediately and that you can select replacements.
4. In A, take three other sessions to Review. In B, submit one of those sessions first and immediately press browser Back. Submit A’s three-session checkout and immediately press Back. In A, validate the email and press Review Booking. Calendar should identify only the shared slot. Refresh to check that the notice survives, then leave Calendar and return. The notice should clear and the other two slots should remain.
5. Try A’s remaining two sessions while B’s checkout is unpaid. A should see one active-checkout notice and retain its cart. In B, resume the original checkout and complete the authorized payment. Verify the paid confirmation and invoice after Back and again after going Home and selecting Book now.
6. In A, open a fresh checkout for the two surviving sessions. Let this **final checkout expire while still on HitPay**, then return to the studio without paying. Verify Calendar recovery, the expiry notice, and absence of both expired slots immediately after release. Go Back to Session and choose sessions again. Calendar should reopen and B should still show its paid confirmation.

Record the page where each timer expired and where the first studio navigation landed. If the customer lands on Home and Book now repeatedly reopens the expired attempt, record the run as failed. Record late payment separately under [background payment](#d--payment-while-the-booking-tab-is-in-the-background).
